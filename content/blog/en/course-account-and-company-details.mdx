Welcome to the first lesson. Before you can make invoices, you set your company details once. After that, you never have to think about it again.

## What you learn
Create your account and fill in your company details, so you're ready to invoice.

## Steps

1. Go to **Create account** and choose that you are a **freelancer (ZZP'er)**.
2. Create your account with your email and password (or with Google).
3. Go through the short **setup wizard**. Fill in your company details: your **name, KvK number, VAT number (btw-id), IBAN and address**.
4. Made an invoice before? You can **upload a photo/PDF** of it and BoekBrug reads your details automatically — saving you typing.
5. Set your **invoice-number starting point** (for example 2026-001). Note: this locks once your first invoice is sent.

## Tip
Something wrong later? You can always adjust your company details under **Settings › My details**. Your data stays yours, and you can export it or delete your account at any time.
