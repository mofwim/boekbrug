Many cost invoices arrive by email. Connect your Gmail and they appear in BoekBrug automatically — ready to check.

## What you learn
Connect your Gmail so incoming invoices arrive automatically.

## Steps

1. Connect your **Gmail** via the step in the **setup wizard**, or later in your settings. This is **optional** — you decide whether to switch it on.
2. BoekBrug pulls invoices from your mailbox and lines them up in **Incoming invoices**.
3. **Check** the read amounts and process them: mark as paid or leave as an outstanding cost.

## Tip
Prefer not to connect a mailbox? Then **Incoming invoices** simply works with what you upload yourself via **+ Add** (see lesson 5). The connection is a convenience, not a requirement. Read more in [Invoices from your inbox](/en/blog/invoices-from-your-inbox).
