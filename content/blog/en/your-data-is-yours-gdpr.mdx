Your admin holds sensitive information: your revenue, your clients, your invoices. It's natural to want to know what happens to that data when you use an online tool. Here's how it works — and what your rights are.

## What does the GDPR say?

The **GDPR** (in Dutch: AVG) is the European privacy law. It gives you a few important rights over your own data:

- You may **see** what data is held about you.
- You may **take your data with you** (export).
- You may have your data **deleted**.

A good tool makes those rights easy to use — not something you wait months for.

## You stay in control with BoekBrug

At **BoekBrug** your data stays yours. Concretely that means:

- **Export whenever you want.** You can download all your data in one go. Want to switch, or just keep a copy? You always can.
- **Delete whenever you want.** You can have your account and data deleted. You're not locked in.
- **You decide what you share.** If you share with your accountant via the bridge, you decide what's shared — and you can break the link again.

So your admin isn't a black hole where your data disappears, but something you stay in charge of.

## What to look for in any tool

- **Can you export your data?** If not, you're locked in.
- **Can you delete your account?** Important if you ever want to stop.
- **Is it clear who has access?** Only you, and whoever you give access to.
- **Is there a privacy statement?** It explains what happens to your data.
