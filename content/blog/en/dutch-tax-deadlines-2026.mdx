Missing deadlines costs money: late with your VAT return means a fine. Luckily the key dates for 2026 are easy to remember. Put them in your calendar and you'll be on time.

## The quarterly VAT return

Most freelancers file VAT every quarter. The rule is simple: your return **and** your payment must be in **within one month after the quarter ends**.

| Quarter 2026 | Period | Deadline (return + payment) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Jan – Mar | 30 April 2026 |
| Q2 | Apr – Jun | 31 July 2026 |
| Q3 | Jul – Sep | 31 October 2026 |
| Q4 | Oct – Dec | 31 January 2027 |

> Note: both the return and the payment must be on time. Keep a few days' margin, especially if the deadline falls on a weekend.

## The income tax return

You report your 2025 income tax in 2026. The return can usually be filed **between 1 March and 1 May**. Can't make it in time? Request an **extension** from the tax office before 1 May.

## How to never miss a deadline

- **Set money aside in time.** Put the VAT and part for income tax aside right away. Then the money is ready on the deadline. Read how much in [Saving for tax](/en/blog/saving-for-tax-freelancer).
- **Keep your admin up to date.** If your revenue and VAT are already added up per quarter, the return itself takes little time.
- **Plan a few days' margin.** Don't file on the last day.
