Retyping receipts is boring work. And every receipt you lose is VAT you don't get back. **Invoice scanning** solves both: you take a photo, and the AI pulls the key details out automatically.

Here's how it works, simply.

## What does the scan do?

You upload a **photo or PDF** of an invoice or receipt. The AI reads the text and recognises the key fields:

- the **supplier** (who the invoice is from),
- the **amount**,
- the **VAT**,
- and often the date too.

So you don't type anything. You just check it's correct and save it.

## Why is that useful?

- **You save time.** No more copying numbers from a receipt.
- **You make fewer mistakes.** Retyping often goes wrong; the scan reads it as written.
- **You don't miss VAT.** You capture every receipt right away, so you don't forget a deduction.
- **Everything is digital and together.** Handy for the 7-year retention rule.

## Do it right away, not at year-end

The biggest win is **capturing immediately**. Get a receipt at the till? Take a photo straight away. That way you build a complete admin all year, without it feeling like work.

## Always check it

The AI is fast and accurate, but it's still smart to check the read-out details. See an amount that's off? Fix it before you save. That keeps your admin reliable.
