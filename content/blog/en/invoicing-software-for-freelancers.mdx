Making invoices by hand in Word works, until you have ten a month. Then you want **invoicing software** that takes the work off your hands. But what should you look for? And can you start free?

## What to look for in invoicing software

- **Correct invoices.** The software automatically makes sure the required fields (VAT number, Chamber of Commerce number, unique number, VAT) are on it.
- **Automatic numbering.** Every invoice number is unique and consecutive, without you tracking it.
- **Send straight away.** Send your invoice by email from the software.
- **See who has paid.** Upload your bank statement and you see which invoices are open and paid.
- **Credit notes.** Correct a mistake in one click.
- **Share with your accountant.** So handing over is no hassle.
- **Your data is yours.** Can you export everything and delete your account? Important.
