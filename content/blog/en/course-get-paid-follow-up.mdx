Sending an invoice is step one. Getting paid is step two. Here's how to keep the overview.

## What you learn
See which invoices are outstanding, and tick off a payment.

## Steps

1. Go to **My invoices**. Each invoice has a status: **draft, sent, paid** or **overdue**.
2. Filter on the tabs to quickly see the **outstanding** or **overdue** invoices.
3. Is an invoice paid? Click **mark as paid** and choose whether it came in via the **bank** or **cash**, with the date.
4. Check your home screen and **Today**: there you see at a glance which invoices you still need to chase.

## Tip
Follow up an overdue invoice quickly with a friendly reminder — most get paid after one message. More tips in [Unpaid invoice: what to do?](/en/blog/unpaid-invoice-what-to-do).
