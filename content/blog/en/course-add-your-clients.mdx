Recording your clients once saves you a lot of typing later: when invoicing, you pick them with a single click.

## What you learn
Add a client to your client list.

## Steps

1. Go to **My clients**.
2. Click **add client**.
3. Fill in the details: **name** (required), and where you have them, also **email, KvK number, VAT number, IBAN and address**.
4. Save. The client is now in your list and searchable by name or email.

## Tip
You don't have to get everything perfect at once. The name is enough to start; you add the rest later via **edit**. The more complete the details (especially VAT number and address), the better your invoices will meet the [invoice requirements](/en/blog/dutch-invoice-requirements).
