BoekBrug is called "the bridge between you and your accountant" for a reason. Here's how you work together, without emailing folders full of PDFs.

## What you learn
Invite your accountant and collaborate through the app.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings** (or use the step in the setup wizard) and **invite your accountant** with their email address.
2. As soon as they accept the invitation, the link is made.
3. From then on, your **invoices and shared documents flow automatically** to your accountant — you don't have to "forward" anything manually.
4. Questions or answers? Use **Messages** in the app, tidily with the right client. No more loose emails.

## Tip
You stay in control: you decide what you share, your data stays yours, and you can always break the link. The formal hand-over you do with the **quarter package** from lesson 8. Read more in [Working with your accountant: the Bridge](/en/blog/working-with-your-accountant).
