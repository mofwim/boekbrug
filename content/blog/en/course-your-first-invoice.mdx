Time for the real work: your first invoice. In a few clicks you have a tidy invoice that meets the rules.

## What you learn
Create, check and send an invoice.

## Steps

1. Go to **New invoice**.
2. Pick your **client** (start typing — they appear from your list) or enter the client manually.
3. Add **invoice lines**: description, quantity, price and the **VAT rate per line** (21%, 9% or 0%).
4. Check the total with and without VAT.
5. Click **save and send**. BoekBrug now assigns the correct, consecutive **invoice number** automatically and emails the invoice as a PDF to your client.

## Tip
Not ready to send? Save it as a **draft** and send later. You can also make it first as a **quote** and convert it to a real invoice with one click (more in the [extra lesson on quotes](/en/blog/course-quotes-and-credit-notes)).

Want to work faster? On your home screen you can describe in plain words what you did ("send 4 hours of consulting to client X") and let the **AI** pre-fill the invoice.
