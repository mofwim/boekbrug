Good bookkeeping software saves you hours a month and prevents mistakes. But there's a lot of choice. What should you look for as a freelancer? And can you start free? Here it is, simply.

## What to look for in bookkeeping software

For most freelancers, these are the things that really matter:

- **Make and send invoices** — correct with the Dutch rules, with automatic numbering.
- **Scan receipts** — take a photo and have the details read automatically, so you don't retype.
- **Quarterly VAT** — your revenue and VAT added up automatically, so your return is nearly done.
- **Your bank** — upload your bank statement and have payments matched to your invoices.
- **Work with your accountant** — share records without emailing folders full of PDFs.
- **Your data is yours** — being able to export everything and delete your account (GDPR).

Don't just look at a long feature list, but at whether the software is **simple** enough that you actually use it.
