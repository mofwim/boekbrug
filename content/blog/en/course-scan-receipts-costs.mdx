Tracking costs lowers your profit and therefore your tax. In BoekBrug you record a receipt in seconds.

## What you learn
Scan and process a receipt or cost invoice.

## Steps

1. Click **+ Add** and take a **photo** or upload a **PDF** of your receipt or cost invoice.
2. BoekBrug automatically recognises whether it's an invoice, receipt, bank statement or document, and reads the supplier, amount and VAT.
3. Go to **Incoming invoices**. Your receipt is there ready to **check** — a receipt is never booked as paid automatically.
4. Check the amounts and confirm: mark as **paid** (bank or cash) or leave it as an outstanding cost.

## Tip
Pay in cash sometimes? Record those expenses (and income) in the **cash book** under **Cash**. That way your cash matches the rest of your admin. Always keep receipts — read why in [Keeping receipts as a freelancer](/en/blog/keeping-receipts-freelancer).
