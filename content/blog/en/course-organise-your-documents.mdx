Contracts, insurance papers, important PDFs — in **My files** all your business paperwork sits in one place.

## What you learn
Store and organise documents in your own folders.

## Steps

1. Go to **My files**.
2. **Upload** a document, or first create a **folder** (for example "Contracts" or "Insurance").
3. **Preview** a file, **rename** it or **move** it to the right folder.
4. Something to remove? Send it to the **trash** — you can restore it later.

## Tip
BoekBrug gives **suggestions** to file your documents in the right place automatically. So your admin stays organised without the hassle. More on smart filing in [Organise your documents automatically](/en/blog/organise-your-documents-automatically).
