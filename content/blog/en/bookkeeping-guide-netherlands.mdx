Keeping your books sounds like a chore, but with a good system it's a few minutes a week. This guide explains, in plain English, how to keep your admin in order as a freelancer in the Netherlands — with as little hassle as possible.

It's an overview. Each section links to more detail.

## What is bookkeeping, really?

Bookkeeping is keeping track of your money: what comes in (revenue) and what goes out (costs). You keep the proof (invoices and receipts) and you track your VAT. It doesn't need to be more than that.

## The basics: everything in one place

The biggest problem is scattering: invoices in your email, receipts in your coat pocket, statements in your banking app. Bring it all to one place. Then you lose nothing and find everything fast.

## What to keep, and for how long?

You keep invoices, receipts, bank statements and returns for **7 years**. Digital is allowed. How to do that easily is in [Keeping receipts and invoices](/en/blog/keeping-receipts-freelancer).

## Upload your bank statement

The fastest way to get your admin correct: upload your bank statement (the file you export from your bank). Then you see every payment and can link it to the right invoice. So you know exactly who has and hasn't paid. Read how it works in [Connecting your bank statement](/en/blog/connect-bank-statement).

## Do it yourself or outsource?

You don't have to do everything yourself. Many freelancers keep their own admin and let an accountant do the filing. What suits you? Read the honest comparison in [DIY bookkeeping or an accountant?](/en/blog/diy-bookkeeping-or-accountant).
