Where do most purchase invoices arrive? In your **inbox**. And that's where they often get stuck: as an attachment in an email that scrolls down, until you've lost them by quarter-end. Every missed invoice is VAT you don't reclaim.

There's an easier way: connect your inbox to your admin.

## The problem: invoices disappear in your inbox

- Invoices arrive as an **attachment**, among all your other mail.
- You have to save and retype them **by hand**.
- At quarter-end you spend hours **searching** your inbox.
- You always miss a few — and so miss VAT.

## The fix: connect Gmail or Outlook

By connecting your inbox, invoices from your email land in your admin **automatically**. You no longer have to save them separately. So you don't lose an attachment and everything is in one place.
