Most freelancers and accountants still work together like this: you email a folder full of PDFs, your accountant emails back with questions, you look something up again. That costs both of you time — and time is money.

BoekBrug is called **"the bridge between you and your bookkeeper"** for a reason. Here's how that bridge works.

## The problem with email

When you share everything by email, all sorts of things go wrong:

- Files get **lost** in long email threads.
- Your accountant isn't sure they have **everything**.
- Questions and answers are **scattered** across dozens of emails.
- You often hand over only just before the deadline, in one big **pile**.

## How the bridge works

With BoekBrug you keep your admin, and you **share it straight with your accountant**. No more emailing folders:

- Your **invoices and documents** are in one place, and your accountant can view the shared records.
- You ask and answer **questions in the app** — via messages, neatly under the right client. No more loose emails.
- Your accountant sees your admin all year, not just in one big pile at the end.
- **Exporting invoices as UBL** (e-invoice) is possible too, so they load into your accountant's software.

## You stay in control

Importantly: you decide what you share, and your data stays yours. You can **export or delete** everything at any time (GDPR-proof). You invite your accountant; you can also break the link again.

## Why this saves you money

An accountant who gets neat, shared records is done faster. And faster usually means cheaper. You hand over more easily, your accountant works more efficiently — everyone wins.
