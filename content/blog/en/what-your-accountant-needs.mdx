Your accountant can only start once you hand over your records. Give them a shoebox full of receipts and the sorting takes a lot of time — and you pay for that time. Hand over everything neatly and complete, and your accountant is faster *and* cheaper.

Here's exactly what your accountant needs.

## The checklist: what you hand over

- **Sales invoices** — all the invoices you sent.
- **Purchase invoices and receipts** — everything you bought for the business.
- **Bank statements** — from your business account, over the whole period.
- **VAT returns** — if you already filed them yourself.
- **Cash records** — if you work with cash.
- **Opening balance** — for the first year, or last year's figures.

For the **annual accounts** your accountant wants this over the whole year. For the **VAT return** usually per quarter.

## The biggest time-waster: scattered records

The problem is almost never that you're missing something. The problem is **scattering**: invoices in your email, receipts in a drawer, statements in your banking app. Your accountant then has to gather it all and check it's complete. That's expensive.

The fix is simple: keep everything in one place all year. Then handing over is a matter of sharing, not days of sorting.
