## Why not just use Word or Excel?

You can make an invoice in Word, but then you have to watch all the requirements yourself: a unique invoice number, your VAT number, your Chamber of Commerce number, the right VAT. Forget something and the invoice isn't complete, and your client sometimes can't deduct the VAT. A free invoice tool does that work for you.

4. **Download the PDF** — a neat invoice, ready to send.
