Because your invoices, receipts and bank are already in BoekBrug, your VAT and result are as good as done. No more adding up by hand.

## What you learn
View and export your figures per quarter.

## Steps

1. Go to **Financial overview** (Result). You see your **revenue, costs and result**, and your **VAT**: due, input VAT and the balance.
2. Open the **Quarterly overview** for the totals per quarter.
3. Ready to hand over? Export a **CSV** or the complete **quarter package (ZIP)** for your accountant.

## Tip
The overview is a handy status through the year. For your official return, use the **quarterly overview** and the export. Want to check yourself first what you owe or get back? Use the free [VAT return calculator](/en/btw-aangifte-berekenen). See also [Filing your VAT return: step by step](/en/blog/how-to-file-dutch-vat-return).
