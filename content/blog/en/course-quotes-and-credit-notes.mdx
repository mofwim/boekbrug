Two handy extras around your invoices: the quote (beforehand) and the credit note (to correct).

## What you learn
Convert a quote to an invoice, and make a credit note.

## Making a quote and converting it

1. Go to **New invoice** and choose the type **quote**.
2. Fill in the same lines as for an invoice and send the quote to your client.
3. Agreed? Convert the quote to an invoice with **one click** — the details stay, and the invoice gets its own number.

## Making a credit note

1. Open a sent or paid invoice under **My invoices**.
2. Choose **make credit note**. This is a kind of negative invoice that corrects the first one.
3. The credit note gets its own number, so your admin stays correct.

## Tip
Use a credit note for a mistake or a return — never change an invoice you already sent. More explanation: [How to write a quote](/en/blog/how-to-write-a-quote-offerte) and [How to make a credit note](/en/blog/how-to-make-a-credit-note).
