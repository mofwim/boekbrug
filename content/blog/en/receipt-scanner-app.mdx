Receipts pile up faster than you can keep track of them. And every receipt you lose is VAT you don't reclaim. The simplest fix: **scan your receipts** right away with your phone.

## Why scan receipts?

- **You lose nothing.** A photo doesn't fade, paper does.
- **You don't type anything.** The AI reads the details.
- **You don't miss VAT.** You capture every receipt right away.
- **You meet the retention rule.** Digital storage is allowed; 7 years of everything neatly together.

## How it works

You take a **photo** of your receipt (or upload a PDF). The AI recognises the **supplier**, the **amount** and the **VAT**, and fills them in for you. You just check it's correct and save it. Done in seconds.
