Almost every freelancer starts with a **spreadsheet** for their admin. Makes sense: it's free and familiar. But a spreadsheet has limits. Here's when it's enough, and when you save yourself time and mistakes by switching.

## When a spreadsheet works fine

- You have **few invoices** a month.
- You have **no staff** and a simple situation.
- You don't mind tracking your VAT and numbering yourself.

In that case a neat sheet can get you quite far.

## When a spreadsheet starts working against you

- You **retype receipts** — that costs time and often goes wrong.
- You have to **watch the invoice rules yourself** (numbering, VAT), and sometimes forget.
- You spend **hours** searching for your figures at quarter-end.
- You're not sure **who has already paid**.
- Your accountant gets a **messy sheet** and charges extra hours.

Recognise this? Then a spreadsheet costs you more than it gives.

## What a tool does instead

Good admin software takes over the boring work:

- **Scan receipts** instead of retyping.
- **Invoices** that are automatically correct and numbered.
- **Quarterly VAT** added up automatically.
- **Match your bank statement** to your invoices, so you see who has paid.
- **Share with your accountant** without hassle.
